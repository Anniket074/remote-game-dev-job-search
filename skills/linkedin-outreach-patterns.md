# Skill: LinkedIn Outreach & Recruiter Interaction Patterns

**Purpose:** Standardized templates and automation patterns for LinkedIn connection requests, direct messages, and inbound screening responses.

**Channels:** LinkedIn Connection Notes (≤300 chars), Direct Messages, InMail, Inbound Questionnaire Responses.

---

## 🎯 Connection Request Notes (≤300 Characters)

### Template Structure
```
Hi {Name}, {specific hook: 1 sentence about their company/role}. 
I'm a {title} with {key metric: 50+ shipped Unity, 35+ Quest VR @ 90+ FPS, custom shaders, multiplayer netcode}. 
Seeking 100% remote {target role}. Portfolio: {url}. Would value connecting.
```

### Used & Proven Examples

| Target | Company | Role Context | Note Sent (Actual) |
|--------|---------|--------------|-------------------|
| Rumman Reza | Baazi Games | Talent Acquisition / Unity Developer | `Hi Rumman, came across Baazi's Unity Developer opening. I've shipped 50+ Unity titles (35+ Quest VR @ 90+ FPS) with deep multiplayer expertise (WebSockets, TCP/UDP, Photon) and custom HLSL shaders. Seeking 100% remote gameplay engineering roles. Portfolio: portfolio-web-pi-inky.vercel.app. Would value connecting.` |
| Sahil Goyal | Westvindh Games | Founder / Senior Game Designer | `Hi Sahil, impressed by Westvindh's casual/physics game focus. I'm a Game Designer & Unity Developer with 50+ shipped titles, 35+ VR @ 90+ FPS, and strong UI/UX design lead background. Seeking 100% remote design roles. Portfolio: portfolio-web-pi-inky.vercel.app. Would love to connect.` |

### Key Principles
- **Hook first:** Reference their specific company/role/post
- **Quantify:** Lead with shipped count, FPS, tech stack
- **Remote explicit:** "100% remote" or "fully remote" in every note
- **Portfolio link:** Always include clickable URL
- **Under 300 chars:** LinkedIn hard limit

## 📬 Follow-Up Patterns

### After Connection Accepted (No Response to Note)
```
Hi {Name}, thanks for connecting! Wanted to follow up on my note — I'm actively seeking 100% remote {role} positions and {Company} is a top target given your work in {specific area}. My portfolio (portfolio-web-pi-inky.vercel.app) has playable demos of VR mechanics, multiplayer systems, and shader work. Would appreciate any pointer to the right hiring contact or process. Best, Anniket
```

### After Application Submitted (LinkedIn Easy Apply)
```
Hi {Recruiter/Hiring Manager if found}, just submitted my application for the {Role} position via LinkedIn Easy Apply (confirmation #{ref}). With 50+ Unity titles shipped and 35+ Quest VR experiences at 90+ FPS, I'd love to discuss how my background in {specific relevant area} aligns with the team's needs. Portfolio: portfolio-web-pi-inky.vercel.app. Thanks for your time.
```

### Re-engagement (2+ Weeks No Response)
```
Hi {Name}, circling back — still very interested in {Company}'s {Role} opening. Recently shipped {new relevant project/metric} which might be relevant. Portfolio updated: portfolio-web-pi-inky.vercel.app. Let me know if there's a better contact or timing. Thanks!
```

## 🔍 Finding the Right Contact

### Search Patterns (LinkedIn Search)
```
site:linkedin.com/in "Talent Acquisition" "Company Name" "Unity" OR "VR" OR "Gameplay"
site:linkedin.com/in "Technical Recruiter" "Company Name" "Game"
site:linkedin.com/in "Engineering Manager" "Company Name" "Gameplay" OR "Systems"
```

### Greenhouse Job Page → LinkedIn Cross-Reference
1. Get hiring team name from Greenhouse job description
2. Search: `"{Name}" "{Company}" "Talent" OR "Recruiter" OR "Engineering Manager"`
3. Send connection note referencing the specific Greenhouse job ID

## ⚠️ Compliance & Etiquette

- **No spam:** Max 1 connection note per person per role
- **Personalize every note:** Generic notes get ignored/reported
- **Respect "Open to Work" signals:** Prioritize recruiters with hiring badges
- **Track thread IDs:** LinkedIn sidebar has no stable anchors — note thread URL after sending DM
- **GDPR/Privacy:** Don't scrape/store contact data beyond this search
